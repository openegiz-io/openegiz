# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone should be able to take part in OpenEgiz without harassment. This applies regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity, or orientation.

## Our standards

Treat people with empathy. Respect different viewpoints and experiences, give and accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, and consider how your actions affect the whole community.

Harassment, sexualized remarks or unwanted advances, trolling, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without permission violate these standards. The same applies to other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and responsibility

These standards apply in project spaces and whenever someone officially represents OpenEgiz in public. Maintainers are responsible for interpreting the standards fairly and may remove or edit content that violates them. When practical, they will explain moderation decisions to the people affected.

## Reporting

Use [GitHub's private reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Choose **Report to repository admins** if offered, or use **Report abuse to GitHub Support**. Include relevant links and describe what happened. Do not share reports or private information in public threads. Maintainers will handle reports promptly and protect the reporter's privacy.

## Enforcement

Maintainers consider the severity, impact, and history of a violation before taking action.

1. **Correction.** For a minor incident, maintainers explain the violation privately and may request a public apology.
2. **Warning.** Maintainers may impose a time-limited restriction on contact with affected participants or on public interactions with the community.
3. **Temporary ban.** A serious violation may lead to exclusion from project spaces and interactions with the community for a defined period.
4. **Permanent ban.** Repeated violations, targeted harassment, or other severe conduct may lead to permanent removal.

Adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its enforcement framework draws on [Mozilla's code of conduct enforcement ladder](https://github.com/mozilla/diversity).
